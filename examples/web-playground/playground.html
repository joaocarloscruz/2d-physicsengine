Controls

        
    
            
  • Drag a body with the left mouse button; drag empty space or use the middle/right button to pan.
    • 
            
  • Mouse wheel zooms at the cursor; arrow keys pan.
    • 
            
  • 1-8 pick a scene, R resets it, Space pauses, N single-steps.
    • 
            
  • C B K drop a circle, box or capsule at the cursor.
    • 
            
  • D toggles the HUD, H this panel.
